MODULES AND FEATURES
====================

What has to be inside each of the four domains for the product to be bought over
what a construction company already runs. Companion to domains.txt: that file
decides what earns a tile, this one decides what has to be behind it.

Australian mid-tier contractor is the reference customer. Fifty to a thousand
people, a handful of concurrent sites, ISO 45001/9001/14001 certified or getting
there, principal contractor on most of its work, tendering to government and
tier ones who audit their supply chain.


HOW TO READ IT
--------------

Every module carries a tier and a status.

  STAKES    Absent and the tool is not credible. A buyer does not thank you for
            it; they leave the demo when it is missing.
  PARITY    The set the incumbents ship. This is where the RFP checklist lives.
  EDGE      Where the product is better rather than equal. Nothing here is
            worth building before the two tiers above it in the same domain.

  [x]  built — a real screen over real records
  [~]  scaffolded — route, nav row, and the right shape with nothing in it
  [ ]  not started — no route and no row

A scaffold is more than a placeholder and less than a feature. It has the
module's own column headings, lanes or axes, under a strip naming the tier and
the one-line summary from this file, so the shape of the screen and the argument
for it can both be reviewed before a line of it is real. Nothing pulses:
a shimmer would promise records that are two seconds away.

The shapes are named in src/lib/modules.ts and drawn by
src/components/features/views. Nineteen of them, which is the whole vocabulary:

  READING RECORDS
  table       a register you filter and sort
  board       the same records by stage
  timeline    bars against a ruler -- lookahead, plant on hire, tickets expiring
  calendar    a month, for anything booked into a slot
  map         where things are -- pinned defects, plant, photos on a plan
  matrix      one axis against another -- risk heat, competency, permissions
  cards       a grid of things identified by a picture rather than a name
  gallery     photos, by the day they were taken
  feed        what happened, newest first, down a rail
  dashboard   tiles over charts
  overview    an app's front page: real tiles, real links, an activity rail

  ONE RECORD
  record      an entity open -- identity, facts, body, activity
  split       a list beside the one it has selected
  documents   a folder tree beside what is in the open folder

  DOING SOMETHING
  checklist   an inspection or an ITP, answered row by row
  wizard      something issued through steps -- a permit, an induction
  form        settings, in sections

  HANDING SOMETHING OVER
  report      an evidence pack, shaped like the paper it becomes
  compare     two versions side by side, with what changed marked

The four groups are the honest division: most of the app is ways of reading a
register, and the templates that are not are the ones worth counting. A product
that only ever renders tables is a product that only ever reports.

A module with two readings gets both, as a segmented control in the page header
— Defects is a board and a list, Risk is a register and a matrix. Only at org
level: the fourth path position is spent on the section once the third is a
site, so a site gets the one reading `siteView` names, which is usually the
narrower one.


WHO WE ARE MEASURED AGAINST
---------------------------

Procore            The gravity well. Project management, drawings, RFIs,
                   financials, a marketplace. Priced for tier one and sold on
                   being the single system. Beaten on price, on time-to-value,
                   and on being built for how AU compliance actually works —
                   not beaten on breadth. Do not try.

HammerTech         The closest AU comparison. Site-safety-first: inductions,
                   permits, plant, prestarts, sign-on. Strong where the site
                   gate is. Weaker as a management system above the site.

SafetyCulture      Checklists as a product. Enormous install base because the
                   free tier is genuinely useful. Anything we ship that is
                   "a form on a phone" is compared to this and must not lose.

Damstra / Rapid    Workforce access, contractor prequal, inductions, e-learning.
                   The compliance gate as a service. Often already in the stack
                   and often the integration rather than the competitor.

Assignar           Field ops for self-perform and subcontractors: scheduling,
                   plant, timesheets, compliance on the crew. The closest thing
                   to our Operations tile.

Sitemate Dashpivot Forms, site diaries, ITPs. Flexible, fast, generic.

myosh / Donesafe   EHS as enterprise software. Registers, workflow, audit.
                   Heavy, configurable, slow to implement.

Raken, Fieldwire   Daily reports and task-level field execution. US-centric.

Cm3, Avetta        Prequalification networks. Integrate, do not rebuild.

Spreadsheets       Still the market leader. Every module below is competing
                   with a shared drive and someone diligent, and losing to
                   those two is the normal way for this to fail.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OPERATIONS  ·  the noun is a job
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Running the work. Everything below has a date, a place and a crew attached.

STAKES

[~] Projects              Register with status, client, contract value, dates,
                          the site it runs on. The spine every other record
                          hangs off -- and the first entity besides a site the
                          nav can open: the row expands to the jobs under it,
                          each with a page of its own.
[x] Sites                 Per-site landing, and a first class scope rather than
                          a filter on projects: crews, plant and obligations sit
                          at a place even when the project list churns. Reached
                          from the panel's Locations segment; the index page it
                          also had was that list a second time, in a worse spot.
[~] Site diary            One entry per site per day: weather pulled not typed,
                          crew on site, plant on site, work done, delays,
                          visitors, photos. Locked at end of day and versioned
                          after. This is the record that wins disputes, so it
                          has to be the fastest screen in the product.
[~] Access & attendance    Who is on site right now, by sign-on. Manual, QR at
                          the gate, or a turnstile feed. Muster report that
                          works with no signal, because the moment it is needed
                          is the moment the network is congested.
[~] Assets & plant        Register of owned and hired plant, where it is,
                          prestart checks, servicing due, registration and
                          insurance expiry, off-hire dates. Hire costs leaking
                          on idle plant is a number the site manager can feel.
[~] Daily task / lookahead Three-week lookahead by crew and by site. The
                          scheduling layer between a Gantt nobody opens and a
                          whiteboard in the shed.
[~] Timesheets            Capture here, rates in Workforce, cost in Commercial —
                          see domains.txt. Capture means: crew, hours, cost
                          code, allowances, on a phone, offline, approved by the
                          supervisor before it leaves the site.

PARITY

[~] Board                 Kanban by stage — tendered through closed. A second reading
                          of the register above, not a module of its own.
[~] Defects & punch lists Location-pinned, photo-evidenced, assigned, aged.
                          Overlaps Quality's NCRs deliberately: a defect is the
                          site's word and an NCR is the system's, and the record
                          is the same one seen from two domains.
[~] Deliveries & logistics Booking a crane, a gate, a slot. Small feature,
                          disproportionate daily use.
[~] Progress claims input  Quantities and percentages captured at the site,
                          handed to Commercial when it exists.
[~] Photos                A real gallery: by site, by date, by tag, by pin on a
                          plan. Every competitor has one and every customer's
                          is a phone camera roll.
[~] Insights              Hours, delays, plant utilisation, open defects,
                          diary completion rate by site.

EDGE

[ ] Offline as the default Not a sync mode you turn on. The diary, prestarts,
                          sign-on and timesheets have to work in a lift well and
                          reconcile without the user thinking about it. Most
                          field tools claim this and degrade to a spinner.
[ ] The day, assembled    One screen per site per morning that already knows
                          weather, crew rostered, plant on hire, permits open,
                          inductions expiring today and yesterday's unclosed
                          items. Competitors ship the parts and let the
                          supervisor assemble them at 6am.
[ ] Evidence by default   Every field action emits a timestamped, geotagged,
                          attributed record that Compliance can cite without
                          anyone re-entering it. See WHERE WE WIN.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
COMPLIANCE  ·  the noun is a clause
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The rules. Strongest domain today and the reason to buy the product.

STAKES

[~] Safety register       Incidents, near misses, hazards, injuries. Severity,
                          investigation, ICAM or 5-why, corrective actions with
                          owners and dates, regulator notifiability flagged at
                          entry rather than remembered. Notifiable incident
                          under s38 is a clock, and the tool should be the thing
                          that starts it.
[~] Risk register         Hierarchy of control, residual scoring, review dates,
                          links from a risk to the SWMS and the controls that
                          treat it.
[~] Quality register      ITPs with hold and witness points, NCRs, inspection
                          records, handover and O&M packs. ITPs are the reason
                          quality software gets bought and the part most safety
                          tools skip.
[~] Environment register  Waste, water, dust, noise, spills, EPA licence
                          conditions and their reporting dates.
[~] SWMS                  Not a document store — a live SWMS. Written against
                          the 18 high risk construction work activities, tied to
                          the risk register, versioned, acknowledged by every
                          worker who does that task on that site, reviewable
                          when the method changes. Subcontractor SWMS reviewed
                          and accepted through the same screen.
[~] Permits to work        Hot work, confined space, live electrical, excavation,
                          working at height, crane lifts. Issued, time-boxed,
                          closed. Expiry visible at the gate.
[~] Inspections & audits   Templated, scheduled, scored, assigned. This is the
                          SafetyCulture surface and it has to be as fast as
                          SafetyCulture or the comparison is fatal.
[~] Documents             Controlled documents: version, owner, review date,
                          acknowledgement, superseded copies unreachable.
                          Document control is what turns a drive into a system.
[~] ISO blueprints        45001 / 9001 / 14001 on the harmonised structure, one
                          clause against every standard that shares it. A row in
                          Compliance's Records group like any other -- it was
                          filed as an add-on for a while, which made a section
                          look like something sold separately.

PARITY

[~] Corrective actions    One queue across every register. An action raised from
                          an incident, an audit finding, an NCR or an inspection
                          is the same object with the same escalation.
[~] Toolbox talks         Scheduled, delivered, attendance captured on site.
[~] Chemicals / SDS       Register with SDS currency (five-year rule) and
                          site-level manifests.
[~] Emergency & first aid Plans, wardens, drills, kit checks per site.
[~] Contractor compliance Insurances, licences, prequal status. Interim home
                          until Subcontractors becomes its own domain.
[~] Insights              Lead and lag indicators: TRIFR, LTIFR, open action
                          ageing, audit scores, inspection coverage, close-out
                          rate by site.

EDGE

[ ] Clause coverage, live The blueprint stops being a reference document and
                          becomes a dashboard: each clause shows the real
                          records satisfying it right now, and where the
                          evidence has gone stale. One control closing a clause
                          in three certifications at once is already the data
                          model — this is the screen that cashes it in.
[ ] Audit mode            Generate the evidence pack for a surveillance audit,
                          a client HSE audit or an FSC audit from live records,
                          scoped to a standard, a site or a date range. The week
                          before an audit is the most painful week of the
                          compliance manager's year and it is entirely
                          mechanical.
[ ] Obligation calendar   Every dated obligation in one place — licence renewals,
                          EPA reporting, plant registrations, insurance expiry,
                          document reviews, drill schedules — with one
                          escalation policy rather than nine reminder settings.
[ ] Regulatory currency   Codes of practice and their state variations tracked
                          by us, not by the customer. Psychosocial hazards,
                          silica, engineered stone, WA and VIC divergence from
                          harmonised WHS. Every AU competitor sells "compliance"
                          and makes the customer own currency.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WORKFORCE  ·  the noun is a person
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The people, and HR beside them. One person record, not three.

STAKES

[~] People directory      Employees, labour hire and subcontractor workers in
                          one register, differentiated by engagement type rather
                          than by living in different systems. Emergency
                          contacts, next of kin, photo, current site.
[~] Training & tickets    White card, HRW licences, EWP, confined space, first
                          aid, VOCs, inductions. Expiry is the whole feature:
                          what has lapsed, what lapses in thirty days, and who
                          is rostered tomorrow with an expired ticket.
[~] Inductions            Company, site and task inductions delivered before the
                          gate, versioned, with a pass mark and a record. The
                          Damstra/Rapid surface, and the one most often already
                          bought — build it, and integrate for the sites where
                          the client mandates someone else's.
[~] Onboarding            Offer through first day: documents, policies, PPE
                          issue, super and TFN, licence collection. Half of this
                          is compliance evidence wearing an HR hat.
[~] Competency matrix     Who is qualified for what, by role and by task, read
                          straight by scheduling and by SWMS acknowledgement.

PARITY

[~] Leave & availability   Requests, approvals, balances, RDO calendars.
[~] Performance & reviews  Light. Nobody switches platforms for this.
[~] Medicals & fitness     Pre-employment, periodic, D&A testing, restrictions
                          and return-to-work plans.
[~] Injury management      Workers comp claims, return-to-work, suitable duties.
                          Sits between Workforce and Safety and must not
                          duplicate the incident.
[~] PPE & issued items     What was issued to whom and when.
[~] Rates & awards         Award interpretation for MA000020 and EBAs, allowances,
                          site allowances, portable long service. Feeds cost
                          when Commercial exists; payroll itself is an
                          integration.
[~] Insights              Headcount, turnover, expiring competencies, training
                          spend, induction coverage by site.

EDGE

[ ] One person, one record The worker who is inducted, ticketed, rostered,
                          signed on, injured and paid is one row. Competitors
                          split this across an HR system, an induction vendor,
                          an access system and a payroll — reconciled by
                          spreadsheet monthly. Holding it once is the single
                          most defensible thing this domain can do.
[ ] Gate-ready            Answer "can this person work on this site tomorrow" in
                          one call: ticket currency, induction for that site,
                          SWMS acknowledged, medical restrictions, right to
                          work. Blocked before the roster goes out rather than
                          discovered at the gate.
[ ] Subcontractor workers  First class, not second. Most of the workforce on a
                          mid-tier site is not employed by the customer, and
                          most tools treat those people as guests.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ADMIN  ·  the noun is the tenant
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The platform itself. Not sold, but lost on: procurement and IT kill deals here.

STAKES

[~] Organisation settings Legal entity, ABN, branding, timezone, financial year,
                          state defaults where WHS diverges.
[~] Members               Invite, deactivate, transfer ownership. Deactivation
                          has to be instant and total — a departed supervisor
                          with a live phone is an audit finding.
[~] Roles & permissions   Role by domain and by site, not one global role. A
                          site manager on two of six sites is the normal case
                          and the case most tools cannot express.
[~] Audit log             Who changed what, when, from where. Immutable. This is
                          a compliance control, not a nice-to-have.
[~] Billing               Plan, seats, invoices, entitlement per domain — the
                          `apps` list is already the entitlement.
[~] Integrations          Connections, keys, sync status, last error, retry.

PARITY

[~] SSO and SCIM          Entra ID and Google. Enterprise procurement asks on
                          the first call and disqualifies on the answer.
[~] Data residency        AU hosting, stated plainly, with the subprocessor
                          list.
[~] Export & portability  Full tenant export. Refusing this reads as lock-in and
                          costs more deals than it saves.
[~] Retention policies    How long records live, per record type, defensibly.
[ ] Custom fields         Per-tenant fields on the core records, without a
                          consultant.
[ ] Templates & forms     A form builder every domain calls — see cross-cutting.

EDGE

[ ] Configuration as data  A new domain is one entry in DOMAINS; the same should
                          be true of a register, a form or a role. Whatever a
                          professional services team would otherwise do by hand
                          is either self-serve or it is a margin leak.
[ ] Sandbox               A copy of the tenant to try a workflow change against
                          before it reaches six sites.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CROSS-CUTTING  ·  services the four domains call
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Not tiles. domains.txt is explicit about why: giving Communication, Knowledge or
Analytics its own app creates two homes for the same artefact. They still have
to be built, and each one is built once.

[ ] Forms & workflow      One builder, one renderer, one approval chain. Every
                          register above is a specialisation of it. Build this
                          badly and every module pays for it forever.
[ ] Mobile & offline      A field app, not a responsive page. See Operations.
[ ] Documents & storage   Versioning, acknowledgement, retention, links from any
                          record to any document, once.
[ ] Notifications         Digest, escalation, on-call. One policy engine, not a
                          preference toggle per module.
[x] Search                Already scoped by domain and site in the sidebar.
                          Needs records behind it.
[~] Insights              A dashboard with a scope picker, rendered per domain.
[ ] Automations           If a licence expires in 30 days, then. Platform
                          capability, per domain.
[ ] API & webhooks        Public, documented, versioned. The integration story
                          is how payroll, accounting, prequal networks and the
                          client's own systems stay out of scope.
[ ] AI where it earns it   Three places it is worth it and nowhere else: writing
                          the incident narrative from the field capture,
                          extracting expiry dates and licence numbers from a
                          photographed ticket or certificate, and answering
                          "where is the evidence for clause 8.1" against the
                          tenant's own records. Anything else is a demo.


WHERE WE WIN
------------

Parity is table stakes and table stakes do not win a switch. Four things are
ours to be better at, and they compound:

1.  One record, many obligations. The blueprint already stores a clause once and
    tags it with the standards that share it. Extend that to evidence and the
    product answers "are we compliant" continuously instead of in the fortnight
    before a surveillance audit. Nobody in the AU mid-market does this well.

2.  Evidence as a byproduct. The compliance artefact should fall out of doing
    the work — a prestart is plant evidence, a sign-on is induction evidence, a
    diary entry is a delay claim. Every competitor makes compliance a second
    job performed after the first one.

3.  One workforce record across employment, competency, access and injury.
    Structurally hard for the incumbents, because their equivalent lives in
    three vendors.

4.  Four navs, one product. The switcher is not cosmetic: the safety manager,
    the site manager and the HR coordinator each see one app, and the data
    underneath is the same. Suites feel like four logins; single apps feel like
    somebody else's job is missing.


WHAT WE ARE NOT BUILDING
------------------------

Saying no is what keeps the four tiles legible.

  Payroll            Regulated, solved, low margin. Integrate.
  Accounting / ERP   Integrate. Commercial is project money, not the ledger.
  Drawings / BIM     The Procore and Aconex moat. Revisit only with Design &
                     Drawings, and only if it is unavoidable.
  Estimating         Pre-award, different buyer, different product.
  A messaging app    Comments on records, yes. A chat product, no.
  Prequal networks   Cm3 and Avetta are networks; the value is the other side of
                     the market. Integrate.


THE ORDER
---------

Ordered by what makes the next module cheaper, not by what demos best.

  1.  Forms and workflow, and offline capture. Everything below is an instance
      of the first and useless without the second.
  2.  Compliance registers made real — safety, risk, quality, environment —
      then corrective actions as one queue across them.
  3.  Site diary, prestarts, sign-on. The daily habit. Without a reason to open
      the app every morning, the registers go stale and the renewal is lost.
  4.  Workforce currency: tickets, inductions, expiry, gate-ready.
  5.  SWMS and permits, which need 1, 2 and 4 to exist first.
  6.  Clause coverage and audit mode. The differentiator, built on top of a year
      of real records rather than demonstrated against fixtures.
  7.  Admin's enterprise tail — SSO, SCIM, export — timed to the first deal that
      requires it, and not before.

Commercial, Subcontractors, Plant & Fleet and Tenders are new domains rather
than modules, and their order is in domains.txt.
